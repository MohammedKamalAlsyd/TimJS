# Privacy Policy for TimJS

**Last updated:** 03/17/2025

TimJS is a browser extension designed to help users understand and optimize their online behavior by tracking browsing habits and providing insights. This Privacy Policy explains how TimJS handles user data.

## 1. Data Collection

TimJS collects the following data to provide its features:

* **Browsing history:** Websites visited, time spent on each website, and navigation patterns.
* **YouTube interaction data:** Genres of YouTube videos and shorts watched (if applicable).

TimJS does not collect any personally identifiable information, such as names, email addresses, or location data.

## 2. Data Usage

The data collected is used solely to generate insights and visualizations within the extension, helping users understand their browsing patterns and optimize their time online. All data processing occurs locally on your device.

## 3. Data Storage

All data collected by TimJS is stored locally on your device using the browser’s storage system (chrome.storage). No data is sent to external servers or shared with third parties.

## 4. Data Sharing

TimJS does not share any user data with third parties. All data remains on your device and is only accessible to you.

## 5. Data Retention

To manage storage efficiently, TimJS automatically deletes browsing data older than 30 days. This ensures that only recent and relevant data is retained.

## 6. User Control

You can manage or delete your data at any time through the extension’s interface. Since all data is stored locally, uninstalling the extension will remove all associated data from your device.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
